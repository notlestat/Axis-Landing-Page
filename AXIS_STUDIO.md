# Axis Studio

## What We Do

We build websites and automated systems for small businesses.

Most small businesses lose potential customers simply because they're too busy to reply to enquiries quickly, or they forget to follow up. We fix that.

## The System

Every client gets:

- A professional website or landing page where their customers can get in touch
- The moment someone fills in the form, the system takes over automatically
- The customer gets a personalised reply within seconds
- Their details get saved automatically
- If they don't reply, they get follow-up emails over the next couple of weeks — without the business owner doing anything
- When they're ready to book, they can do it straight from the email

The whole thing runs itself. The business owner never has to manually chase a lead again.
